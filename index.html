BrandChannelCampaignBudget AMWhy
  • Today is the current day so far. Last 7 Days is the 7 complete days before today. Campaign Duration runs from the tracker start date to today.
  • Budget AM is the full Budget AM of every Active and Launched tracker line for that brand (KD ÷ 0.316 × the client and platform % in each formula). Spend only covers connected lines.
  • Pacing compares spend through yesterday with an even spread of Budget AM over each tracked flight. In tables the bar shows budget spent and the tick shows time elapsed; each brand's ring shows its spend against its full Budget AM.
  • Campaigns are collapsed: tap one to see its channels. Launched, pending, paused and completed campaigns sit at the bottom of each table with their status and date and show whole-flight totals.
  • Results use each platform's own goal: Meta results, TikTok form leads, clicks, follows or video plays, Snapchat leads (swipe-ups otherwise), Google Ads conversions, X engagements.
  • Supplier lines cost clicks × the agreed rate (Iconpeak display and sports $0.40, Landmark $0.40, Premium $0.70) and Shasha views × $0.025. McDonald's clicks come from Bitly; Toyota, Lexus and Al Sayer Corporate clicks are GA4 sessions, which run well below supplier-reported clicks.
  • Red flag means pacing below 80% once a line has run for 3 full days (before that it shows as To Watch, ramping up). Everything else that needs a look is To Watch: cost per lead above $25 (Toyota) or $50 (Lexus), spend over Budget AM, no spend today, frequency of 8+, paused search, lines due but not live.
  • Total Budget is the line's Budget AM from the tracker; Remaining is Total Budget minus spend so far in the campaign (red when overspent). Active and launched lines only.
  • Refresh (top right) pulls new numbers from every platform (about 10–15 minutes; the page updates by itself) and otherwise reloads the latest data. Numbers are also pulled automatically Sun–Thu at 9:00.
  • Brands come from campaign names, so Al Sayer Corporate's anti-counterfeit campaigns appear under Al Sayer Corporate although they run in the Toyota ad accounts. Twitch isn't connected: check its dashboard directly.
  • The data is encrypted. It is only decrypted on your device after the team password is entered.
